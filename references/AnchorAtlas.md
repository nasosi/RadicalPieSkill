# Anchor atlas

Where Radical Pie 1.15.1.0 puts every anchor point, measured index by index, one render per
anchor. Generated by `Scripts/AnchorAtlas.py`, one section per target structure, from the probe
equations it measured. Do not edit by hand.

Positions are in points, in the frame Radical Pie renders into: x grows right from the equation's left edge
and y grows down from its baseline, so a point above the baseline has a negative y.

The fraction columns place the anchor in the box each section names, 0 at the left or top edge and
1 at the right or bottom edge, so a fraction outside 0 to 1 is an anchor outside that box. An index
past the last one a table lists crashes Radical Pie with an access violation rather than raising an
error, so the count is the range a writer may use.

## Bond site: a carbon with a hydrogen in each of the six directions

`Bd` with a `'sing'` bond and a neighbour group in all six directions. The seven bond anchor types box the
site and each of its six neighbour groups: `'bond'` the whole site, `'bdup'` the `'uppr'` group, `'bdur'` the
`'uprt'` group, `'bdul'` the `'uplf'` group, `'bdlw'` the `'lowr'` group, `'bdll'` the `'lwlf'` group and
